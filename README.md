[Blink](https://blinkoften.com/) is a macOS app that helps you rest your eyes, fix your posture, and build better screen habits. It reminds you to step away at the right moments and makes the break feel like a pause rather than an interruption.

Blink learns your rhythm rather than running a stopwatch — pausing when you step away, waiting out your meetings, and speaking up only when your eyes have earned the rest.

## Install

[Download the DMG](https://github.com/hexis-labs/blink-releases/releases/latest/download/Blink.dmg), open it, and drag Blink into your Applications folder.

Blink updates itself from then on — new versions download and install quietly in the background.

## Requirements

Blink requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later, on an Apple Silicon Mac.

## Key features

- **Set your own pace**: How long breaks last, how often they come, how far you can push one back — all yours to change. Blink fits how you already work instead of the reverse
- **Short rests, longer resets**: Quick breaks through the day to keep your eyes fresh, with a longer one every few cycles, for when you need to stand up and leave the desk
- **Nothing lands out of nowhere**: A quiet warning appears before each break, so you can finish your sentence, save your file, or push it back a minute
- **Knows when to keep quiet**: Calls, screen sharing, full-screen video — Blink recognizes them and holds off until you're free
- **Stops when you do**: Step away for a coffee and Blink notices. Timers pause or reset on their own, so you're never told to rest right after you've rested
- **Clocks off with you**: Tell Blink your working hours and it stays silent outside them. No reminders at midnight
- **Choose how firm it is**: Keep skip always available, make it unlock a few seconds in, or take it away entirely for the days you know you'll cave
- **Looks after more than your eyes**: Optional nudges to blink, sit up straight, and drink water — each on its own schedule, each easy to switch off
- **Watch the habit build**: Streaks, a consistency score, a heatmap of your year, and full break history, so you can see whether it's actually working
- **Style it your way**: Eight themes, your own break wallpapers, custom messages, and sounds that don't make you jump — it should look like something you want to see, not a system alert
- **Every display, handled**: One monitor or three, the break screen appears across all of them at once and clears together
- **A glance at the menu bar**: Live countdown, current status, and quick actions without opening settings
